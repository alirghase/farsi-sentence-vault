# اضافه | The ezâfe (-e linking)
An unwritten -e joins a noun to whatever describes or owns it. It is heard, not written.
- **کتابِ من** ketâbe man — my book
- **خونه‌ی بزرگ** khuneye bozorg — a big house (after ـه it becomes -ye)
- **یه قهوه‌ی سرد** ye ghahveye sard — a cold coffee
- **جلوی در** jeloye dar — in front of the door

## Adjectives come after
The noun first, then the adjective, joined by -e: **ماشینِ قرمز** (red car). Without the -e, **ماشین قرمزه** means "the car is red".

## Prepositions take it too
**روی، زیر، کنار، پشت، جلوی، بعد از** all join with -e: **روی میز** ruye miz, on the table.
