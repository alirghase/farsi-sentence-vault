# ضمیرها | Subject pronouns
من (man) I · تو (to) you · اون (un) he, she, it · ما (mâ) we · شما (shomâ) you, plural or polite · اونا (unâ) they.

## Drop them
The verb ending already says who: **می‌رم** is "I'm going", no من needed. Say the pronoun only for contrast: **من می‌رم، تو بمون** (I'll go, you stay).

## تو or شما
**تو** for friends, family, children. **شما** for one person you would call Mr or Mrs, and for any group. With شما the verb takes ـین: **کجا می‌رین؟** Many speakers say ـید in polite speech; both are right.

## He and she are one word
اون is he, she and it. Persian has no grammatical gender, so the context does the work.
