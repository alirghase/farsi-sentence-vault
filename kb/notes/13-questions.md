# سوال | Asking
A yes/no question is the statement with a rising voice: **میای؟** (are you coming?). No word changes.

## Question words stay where the answer would be
- **کجا می‌ری؟** — where are you going? (می‌رم **خونه**)
- **کی میای؟** — when are you coming? (key)
- **کی بود؟** — who was it? (ki)
- **چی می‌خوری؟** — what will you have?
- **چرا نیومدی؟** — why didn't you come?
- **چطوری؟** — how are you? / **چطوری رفتی؟** — how did you go?
- **چقدره؟** — how much is it?
- **چند تا؟** — how many?
- **کدوم؟** — which one?

## مگه
Surprise: **مگه نمی‌دونستی؟** — didn't you know?
