# گذشته | The past
**Simple past = past stem + ending.** The past stem is the infinitive minus ـن: رفتن → رفت.
- رفتم، رفتی، **رفت**، رفتیم، رفتین، رفتن — the "he/she" form has no ending.

## Used to, was doing
**می‌ + past** is a habit or a background: **بچه که بودم، هر روز فوتبال بازی می‌کردم** (as a kid I played football every day).
**داشتم + می‌...** is in the middle of doing: **داشتم غذا می‌خوردم که زنگ زدی** (I was eating when you rang).

## Had done
**past stem + ـه + بودم**: **رفته بودم** (I had gone).

## Has done
Writing has a present perfect (رفته‌ام); speech mostly uses the simple past for it — **ایران رفتی؟** is "have you been to Iran?". The spoken perfect, رفته‌م, differs only by stress, so this vault does not drill it.
